Card banners for version cards.
These 13 files ARE ARK's own store headers (460x215 each), downloaded
from Steam's CDN: base.jpg, center.jpg, primitive.jpg, scorched.jpg,
ragnarok.jpg, aberration.jpg, extinction.jpg, valguero.jpg,
genesis.jpg, crystal_isles.jpg, lost_island.jpg, fjordur.jpg,
aquatica.jpg. They are Wildcard's artwork — NOT covered by the MIT
license in this repo's LICENSE file. They are included for UI
identification in the launcher only.
ark-background.png is the default background image, also Wildcard artwork.
If art/ is ever lost or overwritten, open Tools and click
"Download official ARK headers" to re-fetch these same files
straight from Steam — no need to re-download the whole repo.
